02 / THE APPROACH

Retrieve context, then generate.

Instead of asking a language model to reply from a blank prompt, the workflow uses FAQ similarity search to bring relevant information into the response-generation step.

01 / RETRIEVAL

Find relevant FAQ context

FAQ data is prepared for semantic matching. FAISS supports vector similarity lookup so that review text can be compared against available FAQ content.

02 / GENERATION

Draft a useful, human reply

The retrieved context is supplied to an OpenAI model to draft a response that aims to stay relevant, empathetic, and aligned with the product’s voice. The workflow is a drafting aid, not proof of a fully autonomous publish pipeline.

03 / APPLICATION

Keep the workflow usable

A lightweight Flask interface supports manual review and iteration. The project’s documented stack also includes Pandas, NumPy, FAQ data, and embedding lookup/caching components.

04 / OPERATIONS

Account for retrieval and model behaviour

In AI workflows, the quality of the answer depends on the quality of retrieved context, prompt boundaries, and the model’s interpretation—not only on the model name.

03 / ENGINEERING REVIEW

What makes the output trustworthy?

For a review responder, a fluent answer is not necessarily a correct answer. These are practical evaluation points for the system and for future iterations.

  • Measure whether retrieved FAQs actually answer the review.
  • Define a clear fallback when no relevant FAQ is found.
  • Check for unsupported claims, accidental promises, and unsafe advice.
  • Evaluate tone consistency across low-star and high-star reviews.
  • Track response latency, model errors, retries, and token cost.
  • Keep human review available where customer impact requires it.
04 / TAKEAWAY

AI is strongest with context.

SmartReactor brings together semantic retrieval and language generation for a business workflow. The award is a meaningful recognition; the technical story is how context, product tone, and operational constraints shape an AI response.
